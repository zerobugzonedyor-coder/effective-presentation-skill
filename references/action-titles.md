# Action Titles & Assertion-Evidence

## Rule
Every content slide title must be a complete sentence that states the takeaway (“so what”).

| Topic label (weak) | Action title (strong) |
|--------------------|-----------------------|
| Results | Treatment effect is significant across all three cohorts |
| Literature Review | Prior work leaves the causal mechanism unexplained |
| Data | Dataset covers 40 years of county-level panel data |
| Methodology | Regression discontinuity exploits a sharp funding threshold |
| Discussion | Effect persists after controlling for selection and attrition |
| Next Steps | Pilot the new process in Q1 with three teams |

## Ghost-Deck Test
Read only the sequence of titles. Does the story / argument stand alone? If not, fix titles or restructure before designing visuals.

## Evidence Layer
Under the assertion title, place the single strongest visual or 3–4 short supporting points. The speaker supplies nuance and depth.
