# Story Structures for Presentations

## SCQA (Situation – Complication – Question – Answer)
Ideal for business and technical reports.
- S: Shared context everyone accepts
- C: The conflict or change that creates tension
- Q: The question the audience now has
- A: Your recommendation or solution

## Problem → Solution → Benefit
Classic persuasive arc. Open with the cost of the status quo, present the solution, close with concrete benefits and call to action.

## Hero’s Journey (Duarte / Campbell adapted)
Audience is the hero.
- Ordinary world + pain
- Call to adventure / new idea
- Crossing the threshold (decision)
- New world / transformed results
Emphasize the contrast between “before” and “after” to create the urge to act.

## Practical Checklist Before Building Slides
- Who is the hero (audience)?
- What is the one big idea?
- What decision or action do you want?
- What is the threshold moment?
- Can the title sequence tell the story alone?
